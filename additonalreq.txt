Assignment 2: The Capstone Project (Build Your Own SaaS)

🚨 DEADLINE: Expected to be completed within 4 weeks of the course start date.

Objective

You have learned the complete Vibe Coding workflow. Now, it is time to take the training wheels off. Your final assignment is to look at your real life, identify a problem or a friction point, and build a SaaS application to solve it. You will start entirely from scratch. You must design your own Brand DNA, architect your database, and command your AI to build the solution.

The Core Requirements

To successfully complete your capstone, your application must be pushed live to production (Netlify or Vercel) and must include the foundational stack we learned in this course:

· Authentication (e.g., Clerk)
· A Relational Database (e.g., Supabase) with full CRUD operations.

The Integration Challenge

Real-world applications rely heavily on third-party services. To prove your agentic prompting skills, your SaaS must successfully integrate at least three (3) of the following features:

· Media Management: Allow users to upload, store, and manage images (e.g., using Supabase Storage, Cloudinary, or UploadThing).
· Video Embedding: Seamlessly fetch and play videos from YouTube within your application's UI.
· Transactional Emails: Automatically send an email to a user when a specific action occurs in your app (e.g., using Resend to send a welcome email upon sign-up).
· Monetization: Integrate a payment gateway to accept a one-time payment or subscription (e.g., using Stripe or Lemon Squeezy).
· AI Intelligence: Connect to an LLM API (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini) 
to generate text, summarize data, or provide AI-driven insights directly inside your app.
